## Script 2: "5 mistakes that cost you marks on Paper 3"
**Hook style**: Straight to camera, slightly frustrated teacher energy
**Length**: 50–60 seconds
**Caption**: 5 things examiners keep flagging on AQA Chem Paper 3 — stop losing marks on stuff you actually know 😤 #alevelchemistry #paper3 #examtips

---

Five things examiners flag every single year on Paper 3 and students keep doing them.

One. Writing two answers. If one's wrong, it cancels out the right one. You get zero. Cross out anything you don't want marked.

Two. Describing recrystallisation wrong. Students write about distillation or solvent extraction when the question literally says recrystallisation. Learn the six steps. Dissolve, hot filter, cool, Buchner filter, wash, dry. That order.

Three. Saying reflux is a separation technique. It's not. Reflux returns vapours to the flask. Distillation separates. They are not the same thing.

Four. Vague electrode potential language. Don't write "the electrode potential for copper." Write "Eo for the Cu2+/Cu half-cell." Examiners need you to be specific.

Five. Putting the mass of the solid into q = mcΔT instead of the mass of the solution. It's the mass of the water. Not the solid you dissolved in it.
